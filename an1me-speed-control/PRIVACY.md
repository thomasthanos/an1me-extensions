# Privacy policy - An1me.to Speed Control

**Last updated:** 24 August 2026  
**Publisher:** Thomas Thanos

Nothing leaves your device. The extension has no analytics, advertising, telemetry, account system, or remote data service.

It stores only four preferences in local browser extension storage:

- boost speed
- normal playback speed
- volume
- mute state

The extension runs only on `an1me.to` and does not make its own network requests. Removing the extension removes the data it stored.

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in its update date and in the public commit history.
